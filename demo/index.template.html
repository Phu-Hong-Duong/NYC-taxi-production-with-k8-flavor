Crosstown ETA — how long will this trip take?

A live quote from the model that is serving right now. Pick two zones and a time, and the answer comes back from the cluster — nothing here is pre-computed.

What was sent, and what came back 

    

  


  

    

      The quote is served by nyc-taxi-eta-transformer: this page
      sends the four things a dispatcher knows — a time, two zone ids and a
      party size — and the twenty-four features the model eats are built
      inside the cluster by the same code that trained it. Zone
      centroids and calendar flags come from the online feature store; the
      lookups line below every answer says so, per request.
    

    

      Two things worth trying: a date in 2031 (the committed
      federal-holiday table runs to 2030, so the quote is refused rather
      than guessed), and the two “not a place” zones at the
      bottom of each picker (TLC records them for trips whose zone is unknown —
      they have no geometry, and the model answers from the features that remain).
