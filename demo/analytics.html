A NYC yellow-taxi ETA model, taken from raw TLC parquet to a served, monitored, drift-watched endpoint — Flyte → MLflow → KServe → Feast → Prometheus — and then pointed at March 2020, the most drifted month it will ever see. Every number below is read back from the program's tracked records, not typed.

M3 · The bake-off

Five contenders, one untouched month

Two tracks competed for the serving pointer — an artisan hand-building features, an automated scout×sniper tuning hyperparameters — judged once, on a holdout month no selection step ever read. The answer was features: the engineered set bought +0.56%, tuning on top of it bought +0.07 points more for 2.8× the compute.

Holdout MAE, minutes · Aug 2019, 5.95M trips promotion bar: −2.00% vs the group-median floor
The gate said no more often than yes. Of every verdict this program's promotion gate ever issued, most were refusals — including the champion's own re-fit (−0.03%), a scheduled retrain, and a red-team model fitted on shuffled labels that scored exactly the floor. The pointer moved twice in nine months.

M7 · Scoring 2020 · 15,413,352 trips

91 days, one collapse

The champion — fitted on 2019 — scored every trip of Jan–Mar 2020. Through February it holds ~3 minutes of error. Then the city empties: 240,520 trips on March 5 becomes 5,361 on March 29, a −97.8% fall in 24 days. The streets clear, the median trip runs 49% faster, and the model keeps quoting the old city — the signed bias climbs to +5.3 minutes and never comes back.

Daily volume · quote error · signed bias hover or drag to scrub · arrow keys work too
Bias is the number that reads as a diagnosis. An absolute error can't tell a model quoting too long from one quoting too short — opposite failures with opposite fixes. On March 26 the mean real trip took 9.7 minutes and the model's mean quote was 15.0. The world changed; the model did not follow.

M7 · Drift detection · finding F-045

The average that lied

Ask the monthly aggregates about March 2020 and they shrug: the most-moved input column scores PSI 0.0217 — lower than an ordinary July 2019 — because PSI compares shares, and halving every count changes no share. A row-weighted average of a collapse is weighted by exactly the rows that vanished. Volume is the one marginal that can't be averaged away — so it gets its own alert.

M7 · The drift memo · hour-of-day share

The night ended. The morning moved up.

Same 24 hours, two different cities. Late-March New York loses its nightlife — 1 a.m. falls from 1.9% to 0.7% of daily trips — while the early morning more than doubles: essential workers, moving before dawn. The busiest hour slides from 6 p.m. to mid-afternoon.

M5 · Serving · the kill drill

Kill the only predictor. Time it.

Under a live 4 req/s open-loop load, the drill deletes the model's only serving pod mid-flight. Kubernetes schedules a replacement — a different pod, on a different node — and the route is answering again in 14.5 seconds: 58 failed requests out of 720, then 559 straight with zero errors. The prediction was written to disk before the kill.
